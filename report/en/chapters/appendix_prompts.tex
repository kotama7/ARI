\section{Constitutional RQGM Prompt Seeds}\label{sec:prompts}

This appendix reproduces the model-facing prompt seeds specific to
Constitutional ARI-RQGM. The exact bytes are part of the reproducibility
surface: a governed checkpoint registers their hashes, stamps rendered
uses with the serving version, and stores evolved variants inside the
checkpoint. The listings are therefore identical in all language editions
and are not translated. General execution prompts outside the
constitutional method are outside the scope of this report.
