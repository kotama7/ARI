# Systematic Literature Review

## When to use

Use when a research decision depends on a reproducible survey of prior work.

## Preconditions

The Research Contract fixes the research question, inclusion/exclusion criteria,
time range, evidence vocabulary, and permitted literature-search capability.

## Procedure

Construct query families, use a bound literature-search capability, retain raw
provider records, deduplicate by stable identifiers, screen against declared
criteria, traverse citations under a fixed budget, and synthesize only claims
supported by retained sources.

## Decision points

Expand a query or citation neighborhood when documented coverage gaps remain;
stop when the declared saturation and budget criteria are met.

## Failure conditions

Stop authoritative synthesis on mutable/unrecorded retrieval, unresolved source
identity, criteria drift, missing license/use restrictions, or unsupported claim.

## Expected artifacts

Produce query logs, frozen retrieval snapshot, inclusion decisions, citation
graph, evidence table, and limitations statement.

## Scientific cautions

Provider ranking is not evidence quality. Current indexes may be incomplete or
time-varying and their limitations must remain in provenance.

## Evaluation obligations

Require retrieval provenance completeness, deterministic replay of the frozen
snapshot, decision-trace coverage, and claim-evidence consistency.
